Privacy Policy

How we handle information submitted through this website.

Last updated: September 2026

Kia Todd Talent Agency
Los Angeles, California

This policy explains what information we collect through this website, how it's used, and how it's handled. For privacy questions or requests, contact us through our Contact page.

What we collect

When you submit materials through our talent submission form, we collect what you provide directly. Depending on what's asked at the time, this may include your name, email address, phone number, age, current city, the department you're submitting for, referral information (if applicable), and any files you choose to upload — photos, a resume, a video reel, a voice-over reel, or other materials you voluntarily submit.

How we use it

Submitted information is used to review individuals for potential representation by Kia Todd Talent, communicate with applicants regarding their submissions, and evaluate photographs, résumés, reels, and other materials provided through the submission process.

Where it's stored

Information submitted through the talent submission form is processed through this website's submission system. Uploaded materials — including photos, resumes, and video or voice-over materials — are stored using Cloudflare R2 object storage in a private bucket; they are not made publicly accessible.

Submission notifications and related emails are sent to the agency using Resend, a transactional email service provider.

Third-party services

We use third-party service providers to operate portions of the talent submission system:

  • Cloudflare R2 — used to store uploaded submission materials.
  • Resend — used to deliver submission-related email notifications.

These providers process information as necessary to provide the services described above. We do not sell your submitted information or authorize these providers to use it for their own marketing purposes.

Submissions on behalf of a minor

If you are submitting materials for someone under 18, you confirm that you are their parent or legal guardian and that you consent to this policy on their behalf. Our submission form includes a corresponding confirmation for applicants under 18.

How long we keep it

We retain submissions for as long as reasonably necessary to consider you for representation. You can request that your information be deleted at any time — see below.

Privacy requests

If you have questions about your personal information, or would like to request access to, correction of, or deletion of information you've submitted, please contact us through our Contact page. We will respond to requests as required by applicable law.

Cookies & analytics

This site does not use analytics, advertising trackers, or cookies to monitor visitors. If that changes, this policy will be updated to reflect it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date above will reflect it. Continued use of the submission form after a change means you accept the updated terms.
