Privacy Policy

How we handle information submitted through this website.

Last updated: September 2026

What we collect

When you use the Submissions form on this site, we collect what you enter directly: your name, email address, the department you're submitting for, and any files you choose to upload (photos, a video reel, a voice-over reel, or a resume). We don't collect anything beyond what you submit — this site does not use analytics, advertising trackers, or cookies to monitor visitors.

How we use it

Submitted information is used solely to evaluate you for representation by Kia Todd Talent. Your name and contact details may be used to follow up if there's interest. We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Where it's stored

Uploaded files are stored using Cloudflare's infrastructure (R2 object storage). A notification with a link to your submission is sent to the agency by email via Resend, a transactional email provider. Both are service providers acting on our behalf — neither uses your information for their own purposes.

Submissions on behalf of a minor

If you are submitting materials for someone under 18, you confirm that you are their parent or legal guardian and that you consent to this policy on their behalf.

How long we keep it

We retain submissions for as long as reasonably necessary to consider you for representation. You can request that your information be deleted at any time — see below.

Your rights

You can request to see, update, or delete the information you've submitted at any time. To do so, reach out through our Contact page.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date above will reflect it. Continued use of the submission form after a change means you accept the updated terms.
