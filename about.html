Founder & CEO

Kia's Story

She built her agency on a simple belief: talent, once recognized, deserves someone willing to invest in it.

From Philadelphia to Los Angeles

Kia is a Philadelphia native who spent nearly fifteen years working in healthcare while studying Criminal Justice at Temple University. When friends already working in entertainment saw something in her, she relocated to Los Angeles to pursue talent representation full-time.

A Career Built Around People

Before founding her own agency, Kia served as Director of Commercial Talent and Vice President of Talent at Sheila Legette Entertainment — years spent learning to recognize potential early and guide artists through the decisions that shape a career.

From Talent Discovery to Representation

That eye for potential became the foundation of her own agency: finding people who are ready to be seen, and investing in them until the rest of the industry sees it too.

The Agency

Kia Todd Talent is an agency representing talent across theatrical and commercial work. The approach stays personal by design — a boutique roster, not a numbers game, with a founder who is still directly involved in developing every artist she represents.
