Accessibility Statement

Our commitment to making this site usable for everyone.

Last updated: September 2026

Our commitment

Kia Todd Talent is committed to making this website usable by as many people as possible, including visitors who use screen readers, keyboard navigation, or other assistive technology.

What we've done

  • Semantic HTML structure with proper headings and landmarks
  • Descriptive alt text on talent photos and meaningful images
  • Talent cards are fully keyboard-operable — the flip-to-view-credits interaction works via keyboard, not just mouse or touch
  • Color choices checked for reasonable contrast against the site's dark background
  • A responsive layout that works across phone, tablet, and desktop screen sizes

Ongoing work

This site is actively being developed, and accessibility is something we're continuing to improve rather than treating as finished. Some areas — particularly newer pages — may not yet meet every guideline we're aiming for.

Reporting an issue

If you encounter something on this site that's difficult to use with assistive technology, please let us know through our Contact page — we want to fix it.
