Before You Submit

Departments

Theatrical Commercial Print Voiceover

Geographic Requirements

  • Must reside within a 2-hour driving distance of Los Angeles
  • Able to commit to being in the LA area 2–3 times per week

Required Materials

  • 2–3 natural, everyday photos — no heavy makeup, hats, or glasses
  • Professional headshots optional but welcome
  • Current resume
  • Video reel, where applicable
  • Voice over reel, for the voiceover department

Notes

  • Submit only once — duplicate submissions aren't necessary
  • Allow up to 3 weeks for a response if there's interest
  • Maximum file size for uploads is 15MB

Have a referral?

If you were referred by someone at the agency or a current client, reach out through the contact page and mention who connected you.
